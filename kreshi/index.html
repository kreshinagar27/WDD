Why HTML5?

Advantages
  • Easy access to information from anywhere in the world.
  • Fast communication through emails, chats, and video calls.
  • Supports online learning and education.
  • Enables e-commerce and online banking.
  • Works on multiple devices such as computers, tablets, and smartphones.
Challenges
  • Browser Compatibility.
  • Performance Optimization
  • Security Concerns
